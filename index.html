VK Master Tip: Dry Roasting Spices

Chef's Secret

How to Unlock Maximum Aroma from Whole Spices

Before grinding or adding whole spices like cumin seeds, cardamom, or coriander to your dishes, dry roast them on medium-low heat for 60 to 90 seconds until fragrant. This gentle heat releases essential oils trapped inside the cellular walls, intensifying flavor manifold!

  • Always stir continuously to prevent burning.
  • Cool completely before storing in airtight containers.
